# Encore: 2-minute demo video script

Target length 1:55. Screen recording of the live site, with a calm voice-over. No copyrighted music in the video (the rules forbid it); Encore links out to songs and never plays them, so the recording stays silent apart from the voice.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Landing page, slow scroll past the headline. | "When someone lives with dementia, a song from their youth can still reach them. But care homes reach for the same oldies playlist for everyone. Encore finds the songs, films and shows that are actually *theirs*." |
| 0:12–0:20 | Click **Start the tour**. The comparison screen loads. | "Here's a fictional resident: born 1940, grew up in Mumbai, loved Lata Mangeshkar, Mohammed Rafi and the film *Mughal-e-Azam*." |
| 0:20–0:40 | Pan across the three columns. Pause on the naive Qloo column (Rita Wilson, Charo), then on Encore's list with its year checks. | "Same favorites, three systems. Use Qloo naively, with her age group and where she lives now, and her culture disappears: you get what's popular in New Jersey. Encore uses only her favorites, then puts her youth first: ten out of ten from 1950 to 1970." |
| 0:40–0:58 | Click **Next**: the Memory Box. Hover the life timeline, then one card, pointing at "Encore's reading" and "From Qloo". | "Her Memory Box. The timeline shows ages ten to thirty, when memories stick best. Every card keeps Qloo's evidence apart from Encore's reading, and tells you exactly why it's there." |
| 0:58–1:15 | Tap **Lit up** on Kishore Kumar: the "Plan updated" notice appears and he's pinned. Open **How Encore decided**. | "Mid-session, a carer taps how she responded. 'Lit up' pins it and teaches Qloo more. 'Distressed' removes it for good, and Encore asks before avoiding anything similar. Every step it took is written down." |
| 1:15–1:32 | Visit Kit tab: choose **Hindi**, make the link, open it on a phone frame. | "For family, a private Visit Kit: three songs, a film, and conversation starters in Hindi, Spanish or English, each grounded in Qloo's own descriptions. No names, and the link expires." |
| 1:32–1:48 | Evaluation page: the bar chart and the born-abroad table. | "We tested it on real people: guests of BBC Radio's *Desert Island Discs*, who pick the records they'd keep for life. Across everyone, a plain AI model did better. For people born outside the UK, Encore found twice as many of their hidden favorites. We publish both." |
| 1:48–1:55 | Back to the landing headline; show the URL. | "Encore. The songs, films and shows that still bring them back." |

## Recording checklist
- Use a fresh demo copy (start the tour from the landing page) so the reactions are clean.
- Browser at 1280×800, dark mode off, zoom 100%; hide bookmarks and extensions.
- Check the final evaluation numbers on screen match the voice-over before recording.
- Upload to YouTube as unlisted or public; put the link in the Devpost form.
